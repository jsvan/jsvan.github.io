"You hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of the earth and the sky; why do you not know how to interpret the present time?"

Here is a bunch of lighthearted projects I work on. I start them as I think of them. Some work out, some not. This is what I do when I have a free weekend.

This quote is chosen (I could have also used Goethe etc.) because it captures my ideal projects: in contrast to most work which measures and monetizes the concrete, I want to imagine the hidden currents of the present which move us one way or another. We don't even know they're there until afterwards. Without effort we're stuck with the intellectual and cultural tools of the past and always confused. I want technology to empower us.
