"You hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of the earth and the sky; why do you not know how to interpret the present time?"

Here is a bunch of lighthearted projects I work on. I start them as the thought hits me. Some work out, some not. Some are more finished than others, but nothing is ever done. If I were to have a free weekend, this is what I do. This quote is chosen (I could have used Goethe etc. just as well) because it captures the ideal of my creative approach, which I am unable to achieve: In contrast to most work projects revolving around the concrete, a beauty of life is tackling the indescrible currents of the present constantly sweeping us one way or another. We dont even know they're there until it's too late. Without effort we're stuck with the intellectual and cultural tools of the past and always confused. Let technology empower us.
