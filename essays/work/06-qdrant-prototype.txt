We wanted control over chunking and metadata, and better multilingual retrieval, which the retrieval research had pointed to.

## Design
- Qdrant behind the documents tool, with Gemini embeddings.
- Heading-breadcrumb metadata on every chunk.
- Ingestion moved offline.
- The top search results are injected for the current request only, so they never enter chat history.
- Packaged as a minimal patch that applies cleanly onto main.

## Outcome
Never merged; production stayed on the hosted store.
