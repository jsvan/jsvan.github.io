What do real users actually ask?

## Design
- Parse database dumps for each environment and strip out injected alert messages.
- Deduplicate messages, and key the embedding cache on the message text.
- Gemini embeddings, reduced with UMAP to 10 dimensions, clustered with HDBSCAN, and labelled with TF-IDF.

## Result
60 topic clusters, including several non-English ones. The clusters fed the fine-tuning data and the test corpus.
