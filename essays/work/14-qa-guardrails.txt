QA testing turned up a set of failures. Each fix went into the most general layer that would hold.

## Findings and fixes
- The assistant criticized competitors. Fix: a positioning block that deflects to sales.
- It made up configuration steps for a feature that doesn't exist. Fix: a general premise-verification rule, deliberately written without the specific example.
- Follow-up suggestions went off-topic after a correct refusal. Fix: a scope rule in the follow-up generator.
- Follow-up generation was too expensive. Fix: a smaller model from the same family.
