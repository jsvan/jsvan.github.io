Turn product manuals into good retrieval chunks, with every figure described in words.

## Options I weighed
- PDF needs layout guessing; DOCX has real heading styles. I switched to DOCX.
- Hashing PDF pages was nondeterministic, so I hash the images instead.

## Decisions
- Cache image descriptions by hash, so the expensive vision call runs once per unique image.
- Fail loudly: no partial documents.
- One file per pipeline step.

## Final design
- DOCX to Markdown, with each image replaced by a Gemini 2.5 Flash description that uses the surrounding text and alt text as context.
- Each chunk gets its heading breadcrumb prepended before embedding.
- Chunks of at most 800 tokens, at least 150, with a 200-token overlap.
- 424 cached image descriptions, 340 chunks.

## Outcome
Shipped as its own Go repository.
