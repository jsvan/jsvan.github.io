Life moves me around and sometimes I move myself too. When I sit in airplanes, I wonder about the time-dimensional "snake" that I would look like. I think about what my snake would look like since my birth. This information is lost, but that doesn't mean I couldn't start collecting my data a few decades too late. Every hour my phone saves a location to a growing csv (with some optimizations), but I need a way to visualize it. I had been using the website www.gpsvisualizer.com, but there are two problems: A) I am giving a 3rd party my life location data, and B) its features are a bit clunky for what I need. 

The visualization can be static or animated. Points and connecting lines can be toggled independently, colors changed, opacities, etc. 


This site lets you upload a similar CSV of form:

- one location per line,
- csv
- ordered as date, time, latitude, longitude

- `date, time, latitude, longitude`
- `4/2/24, 08:00:03, 40.4167754, -3.7037902`
- `15.06.2025, 18:00:01, -33.8688197, 151.2092955`

  - Latitude has to be between −90 and 90, longitude between −180 and 180 
  - Dates can be any of the following (if any slash date has a first number above 12, all slash dates in the file are read day-first instead): 
    - M/D/YY or M/D/YYYY
    - D.M.YYYY
    - YYYY-MM-DD
    - Two-digit years become 20xx.
  - Times can be H:MM or H:MM:SS, 24-hour or with AM/PM (with no time, the point is placed at midnight)
  - A line whose date can't be read is dropped
  - Order doesn't matter. Points are sorted by time before drawing, so the animation always runs in time order.
