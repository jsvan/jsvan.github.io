 # Grimmsly - Family Storytelling Platform

My father died and my mother wanted to write his life story into a book for us. She is not a writer, so she found an English Lit. PhD to meet with her, work with her writing, edit, guide, ask questions, and steer her through this process that is full of taste and removing/rising above yourself from your subjective perspective. She loved this experience. The experience was therapeutic, and the expertise of her tutor led her to intellectual areas she never would have arrived to on her own. I wanted to make a business out of this, so this is the website to handle that. I never put in the effort to get it off the ground. Perhaps one day.  

Connect families with talented storytellers to preserve memories in beautiful books, podcasts, and more.

## Features

### For Families (Customers)
- **Find Local Storytellers**: Search for writers, illustrators, and other creatives in your area
- **Cultural Matching**: Connect with storytellers who understand your background and heritage
- **Transparent Pricing**: Set your budget and see artist rates upfront
- **Secure Messaging**: All communication happens through the platform
- **Cloud Storage**: Store interviews, photos, and documents securely
- **Multiple Formats**: Create biographies, children's books, podcasts, documentaries, and more

### For Storytellers (Artists)
- **Vetted Community**: Join a professional network of verified storytellers
- **Set Your Rates**: Full control over pricing and project minimums
- **Custom Templates**: Create project templates for common story types
- **Instagram-Style Portfolio**: Dual system with public slideshow in About section and private Portfolio tab for management
- **Mixed Media**: Upload photos and embed YouTube videos with 16:9 aspect ratio display
- **Multi-Media Support**: Batch image uploads with automatic optimization and thumbnail generation
- **Portfolio Privacy**: Public viewing for all visitors, private management interface for artist only
- **Local & Remote**: Choose to work locally or with clients anywhere

### Platform Features
- **Geographic Search**: Find storytellers by location with distance calculations
- **Smart Matching**: Algorithm matches based on culture, language, and specialization
- **Review System**: Build trust through verified reviews
- **Milestone Payments**: Secure payment system with project milestones
- **Recurring Payments**: Subscription-based support for ongoing projects
- **Story Wall**: Share completed projects and inspire others
- **Community Forum**: Discussion posts with voting and comments

### Administrative Features
- **Complete User Management**: Full lifecycle management from application to deletion
- **Artist Application Review**: Approve, reject, or delete artist applications
- **Comprehensive Data Cleanup**: Cascade deletion with automatic file cleanup from blob storage
- **Project Oversight**: View, manage, and delete projects with all associated data
- **Community Moderation**: Manage forum and wall posts with full content oversight
- **Real-time Analytics**: Platform statistics and user engagement metrics
- **Safety Controls**: Confirmation dialogs for all destructive actions
- **Audit Trail**: Maintain records of rejected applications for business intelligence

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 15, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Next.js API Routes
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM
- **Authentication**: NextAuth.js with CSRF protection
- **File Storage**: Vercel Blob Storage
- **Image Processing**: Sharp.js for optimization and thumbnails
- **Video Integration**: YouTube Data API v3
- **Payments**: Stripe Connect
- **Location Services**: Google Maps API
- **Validation**: Zod for runtime type safety
- **State Management**: Custom React hooks
- **Messaging**: On-demand fetching (webhook-ready architecture)
