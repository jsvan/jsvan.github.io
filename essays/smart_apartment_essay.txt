A satirical art piece exploring dystopian future of apartments. 
"Never attribute to malice what can be attributed to stupidity."

Just as cars have moved in the past 30 years from full mechanical control by a man to fully automated control by a computer, how would luxury apartments develop similarly? What if every manual lever, button, nozzle and gauge were gone to the tenants and they had to use LLM voice interactions to get anything done? For example, a shower has two nozzles hot and cold. Remove them. Ask the AI to turn on the shower. Argue with it until the temperature is just right. Too hot! Too cold! How long would it take? How terrible would that interaction be? Same with ovens, lights, TV etc. To make this funny I don't give AI sensory information to understand what's going on, simply blind access to the mechanical controls. Just like a human has, but worse. 

I don't have access to a physical apartment building I can retrofit, so I made an in browser game. It uses microphone access for the user to speak. The AI is a central listening agent, who then delegates your comments to what it believes are the appropriate appliances. The central AI knows your location and a description and location of each appliance. Each appliance is its own AI, that is, has its own prompt, memory and chat history, and randomly assigned personality from a pregenerated list of 100. Not only are you arguing with the physical limitations of AI intelligence, but also with potentially anti-social or overly-social personalities. 

This is a stalled work in progress. Next is to add gamification to encourage the player to really use the appliances. I'd like health, comfort, energy, hygiene etc. status bars that they have to maintain. Get in the shower too hot and lose health. Can't argue with the toilet to get it to flush? Hygiene decreases. 

To be continued.
